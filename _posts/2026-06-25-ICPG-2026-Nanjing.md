### Presentation

#### Investigating China's Youth Migration Drivers Based on Bias-corrected Social Media Data

**Abstract:** Conventional migration statistics have difficulty capturing recent youth mobility at fine spatial and temporal scales. This study constructs bias-corrected youth (aged 15–34) intercity migration flows from Sina Weibo check-in data across 337 Chinese prefecture-level cities from 2021 to 2024, and links them with city-level demographic, economic, transport and institutional characteristics. Combining a gravity model with migration network analysis, it examines how distance and administrative boundaries, high-speed rail connectivity, the knowledge economy and universities, housing conditions and hukou openness shape where young people move, and how established migration corridors reinforce intercity mobility.
