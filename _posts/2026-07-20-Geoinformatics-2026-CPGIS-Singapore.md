### Presentation

#### Emotional Geographies of Youth Migration: Spatiotemporal Sentiment Dynamics from Digital Traces in China

**Abstract:** Digital trace studies can show when and where people move, but the lived and emotional experience of migration remains less visible. This study links the migration trajectories of young Sina Weibo users (aged 15–34) across 337 Chinese prefecture-level cities from 2021 to 2024 with the same individuals' public posts. It asks who publicly talks about migration, how expressed emotions change around the move, and through which life domains migration experiences are told. The study measures publicly articulated experiences among visible users: the absence of migration-related posts does not mean the absence of a migration experience.
